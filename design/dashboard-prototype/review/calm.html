Scannable at a glance

From the first review: “all of it just looks so busy… I have to read everything all the time.” Each screen now answers is anything wrong, and what needs me? in about three seconds without reading, and says almost nothing when nothing does. The second review corrected an over-correction: calm must not mean hidden, so every row also says what it is, how risky it is and where it lands, with the information kept. Before and after use the same data (the “everything at once” demo day, five things need you). The third column is the default demo day: a mostly healthy day, dozens of runs, one approval and one failure.

  • One loud thing per screenThe few things that need you sit at the top, large, with one sentence and one action. Everything else steps back.
  • Normal collapses into one lineFinished runs, healthy systems and passing lanes fold into a quiet line you can open.
  • Colour only for exceptions and riskFailures, things waiting on you, the higher risk rungs and the “now” cursor. Running work is drawn in ink, the same everywhere.
  • Information kept, identified at a glanceOne row anatomy: an icon for what it is, the title and reason, context, a rung of the risk ladder, the status in a word, the time. Only hashes, raw ids and payloads fold away.
  • Risk and environment always visibleReads only → reversible → changes things → touches production → spends money → destructive, plus a production / staging / local tag where an action lands.
  • Direct labelsMarks on the lanes say what they are (“failed 52 min ago”); no legend to decode.
  • Quiet states are first-class“All quiet. Nothing needs you.” is a complete Overview.
