Bots and Workflows, round 2

These are management pages: the Bots page is for managing bots and building teams, the Workflows page for managing workflows. There is no “Needs you” or “Working now” section on either (Needs you and Activity answer those); a row’s status is one quiet mark that links there. Every row uses the chosen facts line, the names are a realistic fleet, and both pages use the same row and the same browsing pattern. Each frame is the real shell, desktop and phone; open any one full size from its caption.

Already chosen and built: the facts line (option 1, icon and value) and New bot (option A, a sheet over the Bots page).

Bots: manage bots, build teams

Each row says what the bot is for, then its team (and whether it leads it), live or practice mode, what it may do in words, what it’s connected to and its model. Select rows for bulk actions (move to a team, change model, practice mode, archive). Find one at 150 with the filter bar (Team, Mode, Model, Connected to, Folder).

Scale:

1 · Teams are the page

Good
Team building is the page itself: each team is a section whose header says its shared rules in words (the most its bots may do, who approves, what work is routed to it, its budget) with Add a bot and Shape the team, and its members below; solo bots come last, with “Make a team from these”. At 150 the teams collapse to their headers (12 lines), so the organisation is visible at a glance; Group by switches to folders or none.
Bad
A team’s full settings are one click away (Shape the team), not on the page; long teams page by “Show all”.

2 · A teams panel beside the fleet

Good
Teams on the left (all, each team, solo, + new team); picking one opens its builder at the top (lead, the most its bots may do, who approves, routing, sandbox, budget, each changeable in place, Add bots) with its members below. Team building is in plain view whenever a team is picked.
Bad
One team at a time; comparing teams means switching. On a phone the panel becomes a scrolling row of teams.
My pick: 1: teams as the page answers “how is my fleet organised” at any scale, and shaping a team starts from its own header.

Workflows: manage workflows

Each row: an on/off switch, the name, what it does in a sentence, then what starts it and its folder as facts, and a quiet mark for a failed or running last run. Bulk switch on/off and move. Pack workflows sit under their pack, with the pack’s update and Customise; workflows made for bots are on their bot’s page (counted here).

Scale:

1 · Grouped by where they come from

Good
Yours (with your folders as chips at scale), then each pack as a section with its version, update and how much you’ve customised, then “Made for bots” as one line. At 200 it’s a handful of sections, not a list of 200. The same shape as Bots 1.
Bad
Browsing all of yours across folders means picking folders one at a time (or Group by: none).

2 · A sources panel beside the list

Good
Yours and its folders, each pack (marked when it has an update) and Made for bots on the left; the list on the right. The same shape as Bots 2.
Bad
A pack’s update and customisation live one click in, on its own view.
My pick: 1, with Bots 1: one pattern for both pages.
