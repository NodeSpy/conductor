The status column

Companion to Labels and status, which covers every other label in the product. Daniel’s reference: a status column of tinted capsules with outline icons (Passed, Failed, Running, Waiting on you, Parked), plus a full-row red tint on failures. His verdict was “generic”. Round two’s own mocks did the same. Decided: D’s grouping with A’s margin marks, and step detail moved to a tree on the run page. Then made calm by default (Daniel: “all of it just looks so busy”): only the rows that need someone are drawn; Running and Finished are one summary line each. The explored alternatives are kept below for the record.

The rules

  • Group by who needs to act. “Needs attention · N” (failed and needs-you rows) comes first, then running, then finished. A row moves group as its state changes.
  • Normal collapses into one quiet line. Running and Finished are summary lines (“10 finished · 9 passed · 1 parked”) that open in place. Took, cost and ids live inside, never in the default view. In lanes, passing runs are a faint density band.
  • A row that needs someone gets a 3px leading edge in its state colour, its reason as a plain sentence under the run name, and one action. Nothing else in the table is coloured.
  • No tints and no capsules. Status is a small mark plus a word, from status/marks.js, the one vocabulary for tables, cards, lanes, legends, the overview, the run page and phones.
  • Passed is quiet, but never parked. Passed is a small sage dot with an ink word. Parked is slate pause bars. They differ in hue and in shape (see tokens/STATUS-CVD.md).
  • No step trace in lists. Step detail lives on the run page as a collapsible tree. Nested workflow calls collapse to one node with their own status.
  • Every chart mark is explained: labelled in words on its own row (the calm lanes) or listed in its legend, and every mark matches a status token. tools/mark-check.mjs enforces both; tools/calm-check.mjs keeps the first viewport within its loudness budget.
