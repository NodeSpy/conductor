Scannable by default

A correction to “Calm by default”. The calm round over-corrected: it hid information. The real problem was never the amount. Every item looked the same, so you had to read each one to learn what it was and whether it mattered. Icons were missing, and where they existed some didn’t make sense. This proposal keeps the information and adds a visual language that says, before you read, what each thing is, what needs you and whether it is dangerous or routine.

Kept from the calm round: the greeting with the needs-you summary, grouping by attention, margin marks and no tinted capsules. Restored: every row, every column a person decides with. Hidden only: ids, hashes and raw payloads.
