conductor · design system · v1 · October 2026

Calm enough to trust near production.

This is the single source of truth for the README and docs, the dashboard and every surface after them: colour, type, space, shape, motion, components, words and the logo. Everything on this page is rendered live from tokens.css, so what you see is what ships.

The system is independent of the logo. The working mark is Fermata. Switching to another direction swaps the logo files and, at most, the accent ramp (see what changes per direction).

Accent means youEmber appears only where a person is needed or can act: the needs-you count, the primary button, the focus ring and the logo’s dot. Everything else is ink and paper.
Two voicesThe italic serif is the human voice, kept to short titles. The mono is the machine’s voice, for ids, digests, times and YAML. The sans carries everything in between.
State is never colour aloneEvery status pairs a colour with a glyph and a word, so it reads in one colour, on a phone in sunlight, and to colour-blind operators.
Say the risk plainlyPlain words come first and the technical term second. Hashes go under “Technical details”. Before you sign anything, conductor tells you what will change and whether it can be undone.
Fonts: Instrument Serif, Instrument Sans and Martian Mono (SIL OFL 1.1). Icons: Lucide (ISC) and our own status glyphs.Generated from tokens/tokens.json · branch design/brand
