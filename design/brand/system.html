conductor · design system · Daylight · light and dark

Identifiable at a glance.

Daylight is conductor’s design system: colour, type, space, shape, motion, components, information design, words and the logo, for the README and docs, the dashboard and every surface after them. Everything on this page is rendered live from tokens.css, so what you see is what ships. Daylight has a light and a dark theme; an explicit theme always beats the OS.

conductor produces a firehose of runs, approvals, logs and diffs. Daylight keeps the information and makes every item identifiable without reading it: what it is, where it happens, how it went and whether it needs you. The logo is Fermata, strengthened, and it is never altered, decorated, animated or turned into an illustration.

Identify before readingAn icon and its word for what each thing is, an environment tag for where, a status mark and its word for how it went, in the same columns everywhere.
Colour means attentionPassing work is ink. Ember marks where a person is needed; red, amber and blue mark what failed, what waits and what runs. Always with a mark and a word, never a pill.
Plain words, then detailFraunces carries short human titles. Atkinson Hyperlegible carries everything people read. Mono appears only for literal code, ids and YAML, always last on the line.
Say the risk plainlyBefore you sign anything, conductor says what will change, where, and whether it can be undone, in words. Technical details sit underneath.
