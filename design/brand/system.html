How it’s going, in one sentence.

Daylight is conductor’s design system. It is the single source of truth for the README and docs, the dashboard and every surface after them: colour, type, space, shape, motion, components, information design, words and the logo. Everything on this page is rendered live from tokens.css, so what you see is what ships.

conductor produces a firehose of runs, cases, approvals, logs and diffs. Daylight’s job is to make that readable at a glance. Every screen leads with a plain sentence about how things are going and what needs you, and the detail comes on demand. The logo is Fermata, strengthened: the hold-until-released arch, with its kit in assets/.

Summary firstOpen every screen with a sentence: what needs you, what broke, and whether everything else is fine. Tables, logs and hashes come one step down.
Colour means attentionPassing work is ink. Ember marks where a person is needed or can act; red, amber and blue mark what failed, what waits and what runs. Each always comes with a glyph and a word.
Plain words, then detailFraunces carries short human titles. Atkinson Hyperlegible carries everything people read. Mono appears only for literal code, ids and YAML, always last on the line.
Say the risk plainlyBefore you sign anything, conductor says what will change, where, and whether it can be undone. Technical details sit underneath.
