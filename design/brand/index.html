A mark for the thing you let near production.

These are four directions to replace the cartoon mascot and unify the README, the dashboard and the repository’s social card. Each mark is drawn on a 32-unit grid and has its own snapped 16px cut. Each holds up in one colour, and each is shown below in every place it has to live.

Recommendation: ship Fermata

Fermata is the only direction where the mark says something true about the product: nothing proceeds until the conductor releases it. That is approvals, the “needs you” queue and bots acting behind your sign-off. It is also the reason anyone would trust conductor near production.

It does this without a top hat, a baton or a robot. Most engineers will see an arch over a point (a signal and its source, or a gate) and never need the musical reference. Those who know it get a second meaning that fits the name exactly.

On craft, it is symmetrical and calm, holds at 16px as two crisp shapes, and survives one colour with no knockouts. It also sits naturally beside the dashboard’s italic serif, so the dashboard keeps its typography and palette. Only the mark changes.

Conduit is the strong runner-up and the safer “infrastructure logo”. It is the most legible at tiny sizes, but a C with a dot is a crowded genre. Downbeat is the lowest-effort path, and Signature is the most elegant and the least ownable.
