# conductor brand: Fermata

**The idea.** A fermata tells an ensemble to hold until the conductor releases it. conductor works the same way: runs wait behind an approval and bots pause on "needs you". The arch over a point also reads as a signal arc over its source. The mark is an arch and a dot: ink for the arch, Ember for the dot.

All files are in [`assets/`](assets/). They are generated by `tools/build.mjs` (SVG) and `tools/render.mjs` (PNG) from the geometry in `tools/directions.mjs`, so edit the geometry there and never hand-edit the outputs. Wordmarks are outlined, so no font is needed.

"Light" files (no suffix) are for light backgrounds, and `-dark` files are for dark backgrounds.

## Geometry, clear space, minimum size

- **Mark** (32-unit grid): the arch is the region between the ellipses 13×14 and 9.8×10 that share a centre at (16, 21). That makes the crown 4 units and the feet 3.2. The dot has r = 4 and sits on the arch's baseline at (16, 21). The content box is 26×18, centred in the square.
- **16px cut**: below 24px, use `favicon.svg`, not a scaled master. It is redrawn on a 16-unit grid (arch 7×8 / 5×6, feet 2px, dot r 2.1 on whole pixels).
- **Clear space**: one dot diameter on every side (8 units on the 32 grid, or ¼ of the mark's tile). In lockups, measure the dot as it is rendered.
- **Minimum size**: mark 16px (favicon cut) or 24px (master). Horizontal lockup 18px tall or 96px wide. Stacked lockup 64px wide.

## Do / don't

- **Do** keep the dot in the accent colour, or the whole mark in one colour. **Don't** recolour the arch to the accent, add gradients, outlines or shadows, or add a second accent.
- **Do** put the mark on Graphite, Paper, white, black or Ember (one colour, in Bone or white). **Don't** place it on busy imagery without a solid field behind it.
- **Do** use the supplied lockups. **Don't** re-set the wordmark in another face, rearrange the mark and word, or rotate the arch. Upside down it is a smile.
- **Don't** use the mark as a spinner or loading indicator. A held arch is the opposite of "busy".
- **Don't** bring back the mascot, the top hat or the Octocat pin, even "for fun".
