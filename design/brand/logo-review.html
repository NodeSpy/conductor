Round two/Logo review

The mark

Decided: Fermata, strengthened is the official logo. This page is kept as the record of the choice. The round-one Fermata was weak on light, so this page put four candidates side by side in the places a logo actually lives: the dashboard sidebar and top bar, a phone header, a browser tab, the GitHub README, and real sizes from 16px up. Each is shown in Daylight’s light and dark themes, and checked in WebKit on an iPhone profile with the OS in both modes.

Fermata, before and after

On the left is the round-one mark. On the right it is strengthened for light: a heavier arch (crown 4 → 5.5, feet 3.2 → 5), a bigger dot sitting higher under the arch, deeper ink on cream (#1c1712), and the wordmark in Fraunces SemiBold instead of a thin italic. The sunrise rays and hairline baseline from the Daylight motif are dropped from the mark. They cluttered it at small sizes and stay in illustrations only.

The official kit is in assets/; the explored candidates are in logo-kits/<name>/. Each is a complete, drop-in kit: SVG mark, horizontal and stacked lockups and wordmark (light, dark, mono), an inline SVG for app chrome themed by tokens, favicon.svg plus 16/32/48 PNGs, the apple-touch icon, PWA icons and a README with the exact prototype changes. Fonts: Fraunces and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (SIL OFL 1.1).
