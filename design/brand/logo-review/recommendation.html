Daniel picked Fermata, strengthened. It is the official conductor logo, and its kit is in design/brand/assets/. It keeps the one idea that is genuinely conductor’s (nothing proceeds until you release it) and now holds its own on cream in the sidebar, the phone header and the README.

Why not the others

Gate is the clearest favicon, but beside the lowercase wordmark its arch-with-legs reads as a bold “n”. Cue is the boldest silhouette, but abstract: a disc and a dot says nothing about conductor without the name. Conduit is clear and strong, but a C with a dot is a crowded genre. Their kits stay in logo-kits/ for reference.
