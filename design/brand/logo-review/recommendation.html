Ship Fermata, strengthened. It keeps the one idea that is genuinely conductor’s (nothing proceeds until you release it) and fixes the weakness Daniel saw on light. On cream it is now as solid as the other candidates in the sidebar, the phone header and the README.

Why not the others

Gate is the heaviest line mark and the clearest favicon of all. But beside the lowercase wordmark its arch-with-legs reads like a bold “n” at sidebar size (“nconductor”), and that is hard to unsee. Keep it as the fallback if Fermata still feels light in the real dashboard.

Cue has the boldest silhouette on every surface, but a disc and a dot is a familiar abstract shape that says nothing about conductor without the name. Conduit is clear and strong, but it sits in the crowded C-and-a-dot genre and carries the least of conductor’s idea.

What changes if you pick it

Copy logo-kits/fermata-bold/ into the prototype, following its README: the favicon links, the strip mark in js/ui.js and the gate mark in js/screens/access.js, using the inline SVG. The live-text wordmark becomes Fraunces SemiBold. Every kit is complete, so picking another candidate is the same one-folder swap.
